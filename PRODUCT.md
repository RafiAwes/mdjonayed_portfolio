# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Visitors evaluating Md Jonayed for a business engagement: founders and business owners looking for a coach, consultant or strategy planner; companies and event organisers looking for a trainer or speaker; and founders or co-investors exploring tech ventures and investment. They arrive wanting to know who he is, what he offers, and whether he is credible, and they leave by deciding to make contact.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio site for Md Jonayed, presenting him as a business coach, trainer, consultant, entrepreneur, speaker, business-growth and strategy planner, investor and tech entrepreneur. Success is a qualified visitor emailing him.

## Positioning

Undecided. His specific method, niche and proof points have not been supplied yet. Do not invent a methodology name, client list or results.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page site: hero (portrait + role strip), about, approach, ventures, testimonials, contact.
- Primary and only conversion: **email** (mailto). No booking link or CV download for now.
- Stack (existing): Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, GSAP (ScrollTrigger, SplitText), Lenis smooth scroll.
- All content lives in `src/content/site.ts`.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Md Jonayed.
- Visual direction (user, 2026-11-15): modern, innovative, slightly techy (not too techy), with a business feel. Reference: a dark, photo-led personal-brand coaching site. Recorded in DESIGN.md as "graphite & green".
- A professional portrait of Md Jonayed will be supplied (public/portrait.jpg).

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. Every testimonial, approach step, venture, statistic, job entry, email address and social link currently in `src/content/site.ts` is a **placeholder** awaiting real content from the user. Do not fabricate testimonials, client logos, press mentions, revenue figures or audience numbers, and do not present placeholder numbers as fact once the site ships.

## Product Principles

1. Credibility before persuasion: let real experience and outcomes carry the page once supplied. Never fill gaps with invented proof.
2. One clear next step: every section leads toward emailing him.
3. Breadth, organised: he wears many hats (coach, trainer, consultant, speaker, investor, founder). Group them so the range reads as depth, not scatter.
4. Considered and calm: the site should feel like a trusted advisor, not a hype funnel.
