# Surface: Home (`src/app/page.tsx`)

Mode: Persuade. The visitor decides whether to email Md Jonayed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A business advisor with a builder's edge. A dark, photo-led hero establishes presence; precise, measured details (grid, rules, progress lines, mono numerals) add a quiet technical signal without turning into a tech product page. Refuses: icon-card service grids, stats strips, pricing tables and logo walls without real proof.

OWN-WORLD: "Graphite & green" (DESIGN.md): graphite ink bands, cool paper ground, white cards, business green `#1a6b50` for actions on light, mint `#9be1c3` for actions and signals on dark. Geist throughout; Geist Mono only for numbers. 44px pill buttons; crop-mark corners on the portrait.

STORY: Who he is (name, role, portrait) → which of his seven roles fits me → about → how engagements run → track record → what clients say → email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Dark band with a faint 72px grid. Left: availability chip, name at 6rem max, role line, lead, "Email me" (mint) + "How I work". Right: 4:5 portrait with crop marks and a name/location caption. Bottom: full-width seven-role tablist with a mint progress line, summary and "Ask about …" mailto.

FORM: Signature interactions: an auto-advancing role strip (pauses on hover, stops on interaction, off under reduced motion) and an approach timeline that draws itself on scroll (horizontal on desktop, vertical on mobile). User-pinned reference + adjectives; seed: none (pinned) — "modern, innovative and slightly techy (not too techy), it should give a business vibe" plus the supplied reference screenshot.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
